The retrieval problem

01

Clinical evidence lives in both structured records and narrative notes.

Adaptive Clinical GraphRAG brings those sources into one retrieval experience. Semantic similarity can surface relevant text, graph queries can follow explicit relationships, and the interface keeps supporting evidence and provenance close to the answer.

Data + knowledge layer

Structured clinical data meets narrative context.

The system is organized around MIMIC-IV and MIMIC-IV-Note, with Neo4j providing the graph layer for relationship-aware retrieval.
