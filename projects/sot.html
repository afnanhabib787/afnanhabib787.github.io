What SOT is

01

Customer feedback translated into real-time visual signals.

SOT combined hand gestures and facial analysis with person detection, pose estimation, and persistent tracking. The goal was not a collection of isolated demos: it was a customer-facing feedback platform whose vision modules could be evaluated, optimized, served, and connected to application and analytics infrastructure.

Computer-vision modules

One product, multiple perception pathways.

Each module contributed a different signal to the customer-feedback and behavioral-analysis workflow.

GESTURE · GOOD

01 · Interaction

Hand Gesture Recognition

Gesture → satisfaction response. Real-time interpretation turns an intuitive hand signal into structured customer feedback.

FACE · MATCH

02 · Identity

Face Recognition

Face detection and recognition provide a reusable identity-analysis pathway within the broader visual system.

0104TRACK · PERSISTENT ID

03 · Movement

Person Detection + Multi-Object Tracking

Detection → persistent tracking. People remain associated with stable tracking signals across live video frames.

POSE · KEYPOINTS

04 · Body signal

Pose Estimation

Human keypoints capture body configuration and movement as another real-time behavioral signal.

MASK · DETECTED

05 · Detection

Mask Detection

Real-time face-area analysis identifies mask presence for safety or compliance-oriented application contexts.

DEMOGRAPHIC · SIGNAL

06 · Survey context

Demographic Analysis

Customer demographic information can provide additional context for aggregated survey analytics.
